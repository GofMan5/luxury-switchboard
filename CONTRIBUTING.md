# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- **Bugs**: what you did, what happened, what the app said. The version from the sidebar, the OS, and the failing screen go a long way.
- **Pull requests**: read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first — it is the engineering contract, and it is short on ceremony and long on reasons. `pnpm check` must pass; a new behavior arrives with its test; secrets and provider identities never appear in code, tests, screenshots, or commits.
- **Style**: honest states over confident guesses, no dead controls, no water in the UI copy.

By contributing you agree your work is licensed under the project's [MIT license](LICENSE).
