## Luxury Switchboard 1.0.40

**The tunnel answers to nobody's cloud now — including ours.** The public tunnel is a one-click affair: the app downloads a pinned, checksum-verified `cloudflared`, runs a quick tunnel in front of its own fail-closed gateway, and shows you the HTTPS address. No account, no server of ours, no SSH keys, no dashboard. The honest trade, stated in the app: the address is re-rolled every start; the access key is stable. Shared Control and the whole VPS publishing stack are gone.

**Tests you can trust.** The Tests workspace probes with real streams: time to first token (reasoning counts — a thinking model's wait is real), total, decode rate. Image models are labelled instead of red-rowed, and a probe never queues ahead of your traffic — a busy pool says «Pool busy» instead.

**Prices in your currency.** The Insights price catalog has a unit of account: enter rates in USD, CNY, EUR, RUB or any ISO code, and every estimate reads in it.

Plus: every Settings value applies live (no restart, ever), the sidebar collapses, update checks arrive quietly in the pill, and the retry ladder got its public-facing polish.

### Installers

- **Owner** (full product, tunnel included): `Luxury-Switchboard-1.0.40-windows-x64-setup.exe`
- **Public** (no publishing stack, compiled out): `Luxury-Switchboard-1.0.40-public-windows-x64-setup.exe`

Verify against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice — the builds are unsigned.
